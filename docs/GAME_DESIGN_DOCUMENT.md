# Game Design Document for Pacific Rim MMO

## Jaeger Mechanics
- **Control Scheme**: Players pilot Jaegers using a combination of keyboard/mouse or gamepad controls for intuitive maneuvering.
- **Customizable Loadouts**: Choose weapons (e.g., plasma cannons, missile launchers) and defense systems (e.g., shields) tailored to player preferences.
- **Synchronization**: Players must synchronize their actions for maximum efficiency, creating a rhythm that enhances power and combat effectiveness.

## Combat System
- **Real-Time Combat**: Engage in dynamic combat sequences with real-time strategy, allowing players to react swiftly to enemy movements.
- **Special Abilities**: Each Jaeger has unique abilities activated during combat, promoting strategic combinations and teamwork.
- **Enemy AI**: Advanced enemy AI will adapt based on player strategies, ensuring challenging encounters.

## Multiplayer Gameplay
- **Co-op Missions**: Team up with others to take on Kaiju and other challenges, requiring coordination and strategy.
- **PvP Arenas**: Competitive modes where pilots can face off against each other in organized events.
- **Guilds**: Players can form guilds to work together, sharing resources and strategy tips.

## Progression System
- **Experience Points**: Earn XP through combat, missions, and exploration, unlocking skills, abilities, and Jaeger enhancements.
- **Skill Tree**: Players can specialize in certain roles (tank, damage dealer, support), allowing for diverse builds.
- **Resource Management**: Gather materials to upgrade Jaegers and develop new technology.

## Kaiju Encounters
- **Randomized Encounters**: Dynamic spawning of Kaiju in various regions, keeping gameplay fresh and unpredictable.
- **Boss Fights**: Unique and challenging battles against larger Kaiju that require teamwork and strategy.
- **Destruction Mechanics**: Environment interacts with combat; buildings may collapse, altering the battlefield.

## World Building
- **Diverse Environments**: Explore various zones (urban, rural, underwater) with distinct characteristics and challenges.
- **Lore Integration**: Rich backstory that players can uncover through missions, encounters, and collectibles, adding depth to the game universe.
- **Community Involvement**: Players can contribute to world events that impact the game's universe, fostering a sense of shared ownership.
