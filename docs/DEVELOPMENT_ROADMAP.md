# Development Roadmap for Pacific Rim VR MMO Game

## Overview
This document outlines the development roadmap for the Pacific Rim VR MMO game, leveraging Unreal Engine, SteamVR, custom motion suits, and an omnidirectional treadmill, structured around a client-server architecture. This roadmap is a dynamic document that evolves as we make progress through the development phases.

## Phases Overview

1. **Pre-Production (Q1 2026)**  
   - Concept Development  
   - Team Formation  
   - Technology Stack Definition  
   - Initial Prototyping  
   - Project Planning & Timeline Establishment  
  
2. **Alpha Development (Q2 2026 - Q4 2026)**  
   - Level Design and Environment Creation  
   - Core Gameplay Mechanics Implementation  
   - Networking and Client-Server Infrastructure Setup  
   - Integration of SteamVR and Hardware Support (motion suits & treadmill)  
   - Initial Testing and Feedback Collection  
  
3. **Beta Development (Q1 2027)**  
   - Feature Completion  
   - Expanded Testing and Quality Assurance  
   - User Interface and Experience Refinements  
   - Community Engagement and Initial Marketing Strategies  
   - Optimization for Performance  
  
4. **Release Candidate (Q2 2027)**  
   - Final Bug Fixes and Polishing  
   - Load Testing and Security Enhancements  
   - Preparation for Launch (Stores & Platforms)  
   - Finalize Monetization Strategies  
  
5. **Launch (Q3 2027)**  
   - Official Release of the Game  
   - Integration of Online Services  
   - Community Feedback and Post-Launch Support Structures  
  
6. **Post-Launch (Q4 2027 Onward)**  
   - Continuous Support and Maintenance  
   - Expansions and DLC Planning  
   - Frequent Updates and Community Events  
   - Long-term Roadmap Adjustments based on player feedback  

## Conclusion
This roadmap serves as a guide to ensure timely completion of the Pacific Rim VR MMO game, embracing innovative technology to deliver a unique virtual experience and to make informed decisions throughout the development process. 

For any contributions or revisions to this document, please reach out to the development team.
